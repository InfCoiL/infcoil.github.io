What is simulated?

Replay shows one selected qualitative recording per object. The rings turn from red to green when the object center is within 0.3 m of its 3D target and the pelvis is within 0.3 m of its XY target, respectively. Confetti appears once both conditions hold for 10 recorded frames with object speed below 0.2 m/s. Status uses raw trajectory positions; moving outside the tolerance turns a ring red again. The vertical stem and crosshair mark the object target height. These qualitative examples are separate from the manuscript benchmark. In the optional MuJoCo contact sandbox, recorded body poses drive kinematic colliders while the object moves under live physics using a convex hull and a 3 kg demonstration mass. No learned policy runs in this browser; disturbances do not trigger policy replanning, and sandbox outcomes are not evaluation results.
