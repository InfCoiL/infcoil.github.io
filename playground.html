What is simulated?

Replay shows qualitative recordings. The new long-transport clips stop at stable goal arrival; the earlier full-length C45k clips remain available. The two new C45k clips share the same seed and initial poses; earlier baseline clips use different seeds across methods. These selected examples are separate from the manuscript benchmark. In the optional MuJoCo sandbox, recorded body poses drive kinematic colliders while the object moves under live physics. Object collision uses a convex hull and a 3 kg demonstration mass. No learned policy runs in this browser; disturbances do not trigger policy replanning. Sandbox outcomes are not evaluation results.
