What is simulated?

Replay shows one selected qualitative recording per object. The blue ring (0.3 m radius), vertical stem, and 3D crosshair mark the conditioned object goal position; the amber ring (0.16 m radius) marks the pelvis goal position on the ground. These qualitative examples are separate from the manuscript benchmark. In the optional MuJoCo contact sandbox, recorded body poses drive kinematic colliders while the object moves under live physics using a convex hull and a 3 kg demonstration mass. No learned policy runs in this browser; disturbances do not trigger policy replanning, and sandbox outcomes are not evaluation results.
